# The signal chain

```
sources ─▶ (outlets / stage box + snake / DI) ─▶ console input strip
console MAIN L/R, AUX 1/2 ─▶ (processors) ─▶ amplifier IN A / IN B ─▶ OUT 1-2 / OUT 3-4 ─▶ speakers
```

- **Signal only flows along cables.** A cable runs from an OUT socket to an IN socket.
- **Sources**: wireless mics reach receivers on the same channel (1–16) within 128 blocks; worn
  headset and lavalier mics work the same way. Stand, gooseneck and choir mics pick up players
  close to them. Media decks and the console's USB player play files. The video capture device
  takes WATERFrAMES screens in its zone.
- **Console**: each input strip applies gain, 3-band EQ, its fader and pan, and sends to the
  buses: **MAIN L**, **MAIN R**, **AUX 1**, **AUX 2**, and the effects unit (an echo, returned to
  MAIN). Mute cuts the strip.
- **Processors** (graphic EQ, compressor, effects, crossover) sit in a cable run and change the
  sound on its way.
- **Amplifier**: **IN A** feeds **OUT 1–2**, **IN B** feeds **OUT 3–4**, at the level knob's gain;
  off when its power switch is off. So MAIN L → IN A and MAIN R → IN B gives stereo.
- **Speakers** play what reaches them through Simple Voice Chat, heard from where they stand,
  within their coverage (16–128 blocks, set on their back). In-ear monitors play only to the
  player wearing the earpiece.

Every player hears the speakers from their own position, so a stereo pair sounds left and right.
