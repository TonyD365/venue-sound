# Devices

Recipes, placing and every socket of each item: [every item and block](items.md).

Controls are used on the model: right-click buttons and switches, hold right-click on a fader
and move the crosshair (or scroll), click the left/right half of a knob (or scroll) to turn it.
Sockets show blue (IN) or red (OUT) rings with labels.

## Sources

| Device | Use | Sockets |
|---|---|---|
| Wireless Microphone | Hold to talk on its channel (1–16). Right-click: channel; sneak + right-click: mute | — |
| Headset Mic, Lavalier Mic | Right-click to wear (head / chest); it transmits while worn or carried. Sneak + right-click: next channel (set it to the receiver's channel) | — |
| Wireless Receiver | Picks up mics on its channel within 128 blocks. Click the display or -/+ | OUT (back) |
| Stand / Gooseneck / Choir Mic | Pick up players near them (choir mic: a wider area) | OUT |
| Media Deck | Prev / play / stop / next / loop on the front, display shows the track | OUT (back) |
| Video Capture + Zone Selector | Select an area with WATERFrAMES screens, then click the capture device | OUT |

## Rack

| Device | Use |
|---|---|
| Power Amplifier | Power rocker and level knob on the front; IN A/B and OUT 1–4 on the back |
| Graphic Equalizer, Compressor / Limiter, Effects Processor, Crossover | In-line processors: cable them between the console and the amplifier |
| Power Sequencer | Switches amplifiers and speakers nearby on and off in order |
| Rack Cabinet | Holds rack gear |

## Speakers and monitors

| Device | Notes |
|---|---|
| Line Array Module, Flown Line Array + Truss Hanging Point | Compact; hang them from a truss |
| PA Speaker Cabinet | Two blocks tall |
| Concert Speaker Stack | 4×6×2 blocks; horns, 15\" drivers, 18\" subwoofers |
| Floor Wedge Monitor, Subwoofer | Stage monitor; low end |
| In-Ear Monitor Transmitter + Earpiece | Only the player wearing the earpiece hears it |

Every speaker has INPUT, a power switch and a coverage knob (16–128 blocks) on its back.
