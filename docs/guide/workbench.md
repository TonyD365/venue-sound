# Workbench

A desktop app with the same devices, controls and signal engine as the mod.

```bash
java -jar venuesound-workbench.jar
```

- **Select**: click a device; its panel opens on the right (faders, knobs, buttons). Drag the
  blue marker (you) around the floor.
- **Cable**: click a device to pick an OUT socket, click clips to route, then a device's IN socket.
  Right-click a device to unplug its cables.
- **Place / Rotate (R) / Delete**.
- **🎤 Talk**: your computer's microphone acts as the wireless mic (set its channel) or a stand
  mic within 3 blocks. **Use headphones** to avoid feedback.
- **Media folder…**: choose a folder of mp3/wav files for the media deck and USB player.

You hear the speakers in stereo from where the marker stands.
