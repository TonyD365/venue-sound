Devices

Sources
Wireless mic (16 channels), headset and lavalier mics you wear, stand, gooseneck and choir mics, media deck, video capture for WATERFrAMES screens.
Consoles
VS-12 (8 + 2 stereo, 3×2 blocks) and VS-24 (16 + 4, 5×2 blocks): gain, 3-band EQ, AUX, effects, pan, mute, faders, meters.
Rack
Power amplifier, graphic EQ, compressor, effects processor, crossover, power sequencer, rack cabinet.
Speakers
Line arrays (also flown from trusses), PA cabinets, a 4×6 concert stack, floor wedges, subwoofers, in-ear monitors.
Cabling
XLR, speaker cable, multicore snake with 8/16-channel stage boxes, wall outlets, DI box, cable clips. Cables can't go through walls.

Quick start

  1. Install Fabric Loader for Minecraft 1.20.1, Fabric API and Simple Voice Chat, and put VenueSound in mods/.
  2. Place a mixing console, a power amplifier and two PA speakers.
  3. With an audio cable: console MAIN L → IN A, MAIN R → IN B on the amplifier; amplifier OUT 1 and OUT 3 → each speaker's INPUT.
  4. Cable a wireless receiver's OUT to console IN 1.
  5. Hold a wireless mic on the receiver's channel and talk. Push the faders up.

Workbench

Not in Minecraft? The Workbench desktop app has the same devices, controls and signal engine. Build a system, run cables, drag faders — and hear it from your computer's microphone and music files, in stereo.

java -jar venuesound-workbench.jar
