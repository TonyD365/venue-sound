# Getting help

1. **Read the guide.** The [user guide](docs/guide/README.md) covers installing, the signal chain,
   every device and cabling. The [FAQ](README.md#faq) answers the most common questions.
2. **Check [troubleshooting](docs/guide/troubleshooting.md)** for "no sound" and refused cables.
3. **Search the [issues](https://github.com/TonyD365/venue-sound/issues?q=is%3Aissue)**: someone
   may have had the same problem.
4. **Open an issue** with the [form](https://github.com/TonyD365/venue-sound/issues/new/choose).
   Your VenueSound and Simple Voice Chat versions and `logs/latest.log` make it much quicker to
   help. English or Chinese are both fine.

Security problems are different: see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
